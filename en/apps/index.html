Apps

Seven platforms, one set of clouds

The same core underneath, shaped to each device: a file manager in your pocket on a phone, a remote-driven media centre on a TV, a private cinema in a headset.

  • iPhone and iPad

    Every cloud you own, in your pocket. Stream Blu-ray ISOs, Dolby Vision and DSD music straight from the cloud, with Files app integration and a Columns view on iPad.

    On the App Store

  • Mac

    A native Mac app with a Finder-style Columns view and full keyboard control, and it can mount your clouds as local disks for everything else on the machine.

    On the App Store · direct download available

  • Apple TV

    Your cloud media centre in the living room. No downloads and no NAS — disc menus, Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos go straight to the big screen.

    On the App Store

  • Apple Vision Pro

    A private cinema you wear. 180°/360° VR, Apple Spatial video and MVC 3D Blu-ray, all played straight from the cloud.

    On the App Store

  • Android phone and Android TV

    All your clouds plus an audiophile-grade player: real disc menus (HDMV and BD-J), Dolby Vision, DSD and SACD. Install it on a TV box and it switches to a remote-friendly interface.

    On Google Play

  • Meta Quest

    Your clouds inside the headset. Up to 8K hardware decoding, Blu-ray and DVD ISOs read in place, 360 and VR180, passthrough VR and native DSD1024 output.

    Available through the Meta BETA channel

  • Windows

    A native Windows app that mounts your clouds as a drive letter in File Explorer, with full NTFS semantics and security descriptors. Blu-ray disc menus (HDMV and BD-J), true Dolby Vision and DSD1024 are here too.

    Direct download · x64 and Arm64

What runs on Linux, a NAS or Docker is the core service, not an app — no interface of its own, sitting on the machine that stays on, managed from a browser.

How the apps differ

The same core ships inside every app, so everything to do with clouds and files behaves the same everywhere. The differences are concentrated in mounting, which the operating system has to allow, and in audio and video output, which depends on hardware and drivers.

1.2.0 public beta

App 1.2.0 public beta

These features are in the 1.2.0 public beta. Use CloudDrive2 App 1.2.0-beta or later with a 1.2.0-beta or later media server. Downloads and beta channels.

  • Connect to a media server

    Let a computer or NAS scan films, shows, music and books, then pair the app to browse with viewer profiles, favorites and Continue Watching. The app can also serve the libraries organized on this device.

  • Library browsing and metadata

    Filter by video, HDR, audio and subtitle availability, and browse related works on people pages. Read local season NFO files and posters, including a poster for a single-season show. Audio and subtitle choices follow each title.

  • Background AI subtitles

    Submit films, episodes or whole seasons for subtitle generation and translation. View processing time, pause, resume and reorder waiting jobs. A connected media server processes the jobs without playing the video.

  • Players and readers

    Video adds A–B repeat and total/remaining time. Music brings lyrics, queues and album information together. Read CBR comics and view still or animated JPEG XL images.

  • Platform updates

    iPhone Duo fold layouts, Mac menu bar mode, a Windows music window, TV remote improvements, and listening rooms and home theater on Vision Pro and Quest.

  • Independent web player

    The core’s browser interface decodes almost all audio and video formats without server-side transcoding or enabling the media server. Support and performance depend on the browser and device.

Connecting to a server requires App Pro. Serving this device’s libraries requires App Pro and an eligible core plan (monthly, yearly or full Lifetime; Lifetime Lite is excluded). Keep the serving device running; background availability depends on the operating system. Membership details

One account

Buy once, use everywhere

Membership follows your account

A CloudDrive2 membership is tied to your CloudDrive account, not to a device or a store. Sign in on a machine and the membership applies there — Windows, Linux, NAS, Android, Apple devices and Meta Quest all alike, with no exceptions.

App Pro follows your account too

Browsing clouds, managing files and basic playback need no extra purchase. The advanced playback features — HDR and Dolby Vision, DSD, ISO disc playback (Blu-ray, DVD and SACD), AI subtitle translation, the media client (Emby and Jellyfin), the media library, and VR video playback of every kind (VR180, 360, fisheye, EAC, 3D SBS/OU) — need the one-time App Pro in-app purchase. Buy it once and it is unlocked on every device that signs in with the same CloudDrive account. Nothing to buy again, and nothing counted per device or per platform.

Where you buy makes no difference

An app installed from the App Store, Google Play or the Meta store buys through that store; the Windows app and the macOS app or Android APK downloaded from this site buy directly with your CloudDrive account. Either way the entitlement ends up on the same CloudDrive account, and signing in to another build with that account unlocks it there too.

Store purchases show the price in your region's local currency, which may differ from the yuan list price. The price shown in the app is the one that applies.

After changing devices or reinstalling, sign in with the same account — there is nothing to buy a second time.
