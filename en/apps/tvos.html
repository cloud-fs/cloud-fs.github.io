Media

Cinema-grade playback, straight from the cloud

A 60 GB disc image never has to be downloaded first, and you don't need a NAS to serve it. The player reads from the cloud, and decoding, menus, audio tracks and subtitles all come with it.

Cinema-grade playback and Blu-ray ISOs

Hardware-accelerated H.264, H.265/HEVC and AV1 decoding with HDR10, HDR10+ output signalling and Dolby Vision — including true Dolby Vision playback of Profile 7 Blu-ray content. Frame-rate matching keeps motion judder-free, the scrubber shows thumbnail previews, and playback resumes where you left off.

Blu-ray ISOs play directly from cloud storage and open the native disc menu: scene selection, coloured shortcut keys on the remote, in-menu audio and subtitle setup, and 2D+3D combo discs.

Disc menus cover HDMV discs. On Apple platforms a BD-J (Java) disc plays the main feature directly; for BD-J menus, see the Android app.

  • Dolby Vision P5 / 7 / 8
  • HDR10+ · frame-rate match
  • HDMV menus
  • 2D + 3D combo discs

Subtitles done right

SRT, ASS/SSA, VTT and PGS bitmap subtitles, with styling, per-track delay and HDR brightness control.

AI subtitle translation on Apple TV runs through your own API. The easier route is to generate the subtitles on your iPhone or Mac first — they are saved next to the video in your cloud, so Apple TV picks them up when you open the same file and nothing has to be re-run on the TV.

tvOS has no Apple on-device translation models, so AI subtitles here require your own API; use the on-device models on iPhone, iPad or Mac.

  • Bring your own API
  • Reused across devices
  • PGS bitmap subtitles
  • HDR subtitle brightness

Lossless music, Dolby Atmos and multichannel surround

A full audio player on your Apple TV: FLAC, WAV, ALAC, APE, DSD up to DSD1024 and SACD ISO playback. Album artwork, gapless playback and background audio — music keeps playing while you browse, with Now Playing controls on your remote.

Dolby Atmos (Dolby Digital Plus) bitstreams straight to your AVR or soundbar over HDMI. TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio and DTS decode in full and go out over HDMI as 5.1 or 7.1 — never folded down to stereo. Multichannel DSD does the same: 5.1 SACD ISO, .dsf and .dff play in true surround through your receiver. The full surround mix is always preserved, so AirPods Pro and Max get head-tracked spatial audio and stereo setups get a proper ITU-R BS.775 downmix.

  • DSD64 → DSD1024
  • SACD ISO
  • Atmos bitstream
  • Background playback

A multi-gigabyte PDF, open in seconds

The streaming PDF reader never pulls the whole book down. It fetches only the byte range the pages on screen actually need, so a multi-gigabyte art book typically goes from click to first page in seconds — no waiting for a download, and no complete copy left on the Apple TV.

You read it full-screen, with paging and jumps adapted to the remote and everything fetched on demand, and your reading position is remembered so the next time you open it you carry on from the same page.

  • Fetches pages on demand
  • No full download
  • Remote-friendly

More

The rest of what you'll use daily

  • Cloud media center

    Google Drive, OneDrive, Aliyun Drive, Baidu Pan, 115, S3, WebDAV and more, all in one place. Browse them from the couch with a TV interface built for the Siri Remote: grid and list views, thumbnail previews and fast navigation through folders with thousands of files.

  • Emby and Jellyfin client

    Connect your own Emby or Jellyfin servers and browse libraries with posters, cast and Continue Watching. When your CloudDrive can reach the same file, Direct Stream plays it straight from the cloud, bypassing the server transcoder, with watch history kept in sync.

  • Movie library metadata

    Reads your local .nfo files and poster artwork in Plex, Jellyfin and Emby naming for a rich, private library — no online scraping.

  • Security

    Sign in with your CloudDrive account; two-factor authentication and active session monitoring keep your data safe, and API tokens grant scoped, expiring access.

  • A WebDAV server of its own

    Your Apple TV is itself a WebDAV server: every cloud you have connected is served over a standard protocol, so third-party apps on the same network — players, file managers, backup tools — read your cloud content directly without signing in to each provider again.

  • And these

    Grid and list views a click apart, thumbnail seek preview and resume playback, Now Playing controls on the remote, dark and light themes, English and Chinese interfaces.
