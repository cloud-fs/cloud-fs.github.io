Clouds

Unified cloud management

Every cloud you own, in one space

Google Drive, OneDrive, Aliyun Drive, Baidu Pan, 115, S3, WebDAV, SFTP, FTP, SMB and more, all connected in one place. Browse, search, copy, move, rename and organise files across clouds; cross-cloud transfers pause and resume, and automated backup tasks, recursive folder upload and a streaming PDF reader are in the same app.

A full File Provider extension puts your cloud files inside the visionOS Files app and any app that uses the document picker. Lay the windows out side by side and sort files while a movie keeps playing.

  • Search across clouds
  • S3 · WebDAV · SMB
  • File Provider

Media

From flat movies to fully immersive VR

A 60 GB disc image never has to come down first. The player reads from the cloud, and decoding, menus, audio tracks and subtitles all come with it.

In an immersive space you can watch 180° and 360° VR video, Apple Spatial (MV-HEVC) video, side-by-side and over-under stereo 3D, fisheye and EAC cubemap content, with passthrough for comfort. Head-locked subtitles follow your view with automatic depth matching — comfortable to read even on very close objects.

Cinema-grade flat playback and Blu-ray ISOs

H.264, H.265/HEVC and AV1 — including real-time 8K AV1 playback — with HDR10, HDR10+ and Dolby Vision, including true Dolby Vision playback of Profile 7 Blu-ray content. Make the screen as large as you like; wherever you sit, you are sitting in the middle.

Play a Blu-ray ISO straight from cloud storage and navigate the native disc menu: scene selection, coloured shortcut keys, in-menu audio and subtitle setup, even 2D+3D combo discs. MVC 3D Blu-ray inside an ISO decodes both eye streams natively and stays stereoscopic across seeks, chapters and clip changes.

Disc menus cover HDMV discs. On Apple platforms a BD-J (Java) disc plays the main feature directly; for BD-J menus, see the Android app.

  • 8K AV1
  • Dolby Vision P5 / 7 / 8
  • HDMV menus
  • MVC 3D Blu-ray

AI subtitle translation

Generate translated subtitles for any video. CloudDrive2 translates the existing subtitle track first — including PGS bitmap subtitles read by on-device OCR — and falls back to speech recognition only when there is none. Use Apple's on-device translation models, which are free and keep the audio on your device, or bring your own API.

Generated subtitles are saved next to the video, ready to reuse on your other devices, and they follow your view in immersive mode too.

  • Apple on-device models
  • PGS OCR
  • Saved for reuse

Multichannel and audiophile-grade audio

Dolby Atmos, TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio and DTS soundtracks decode in full 5.1 or 7.1 and render as head-tracked spatial audio — the whole surround bed placed around you, never a stereo fold-down. It works through Vision Pro's own audio and with AirPods Pro and Max.

Multichannel DSD does the same: 5.1 SACD ISO, .dsf and .dff play in true surround. Plus FLAC, WAV and ALAC, and DSD playback up to DSD1024 with DoP output.

  • DSD64 → DSD1024
  • Multichannel SACD ISO
  • TrueHD · DTS-HD MA
  • Head-tracked spatial audio

A multi-gigabyte PDF, open in seconds

The streaming PDF reader never pulls the whole book down. It fetches only the byte range the pages on screen actually need, so a multi-gigabyte art book typically goes from tap to first page in seconds — no waiting for a download, and no complete copy left behind.

The reader panel floats in your space alongside everything else, at whatever size suits you. Turning pages, zooming and jumping through the outline all fetch on demand, with page thumbnails, bookmarks and reading-position memory, so another device signed in to the same CloudDrive account picks up where you left off.

  • Fetches pages on demand
  • No full download
  • Floating panel
  • Bookmarks and position

More

The rest of what you'll use daily

  • Emby and Jellyfin client

    Connect your own Emby or Jellyfin servers and browse libraries with posters, cast and Continue Watching. When your CloudDrive can reach the same file, Direct Stream plays it straight from the cloud at full quality — including formats servers can't transcode — while watch history stays in sync.

  • Immersive formats supported

    180° and 360° VR video, Apple Spatial (MV-HEVC) video, side-by-side and over-under stereo 3D, fisheye, EAC cubemap, and MVC 3D Blu-ray from an ISO. Passthrough stays available while you watch.

  • Transfers and backup

    Copy or move files between providers, with transfers you can pause and resume. Automated backup tasks run on a schedule, and whole folders upload recursively.

  • Sharing and security

    Two-factor authentication, encrypted folders and active session monitoring. The built-in WebDAV server shares your cloud files with other devices on the same network, and API tokens grant scoped, expiring access.

  • And these

    Library metadata from local .nfo files and artwork in Plex, Jellyfin and Emby naming (no online scraping), disk cache with configurable eviction, independent audio and subtitle delay, dark and light themes, English and Chinese interfaces.
