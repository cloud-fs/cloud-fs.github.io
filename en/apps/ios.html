Columns and an inspector on iPad

iPad gets a Finder-style Columns view and a file inspector panel. With a keyboard attached you can drill through a large library one column at a time and read a file's properties without leaving the list. Four view modes — list, grid, gallery and columns — are a tap apart.

  • Columns view
  • Inspector panel
  • Gallery thumbnails

Built into the Files app

A full File Provider extension puts your cloud files inside the iOS and iPadOS Files app, and inside any app that uses the document picker. CloudDrive2 can also act as an external player for other apps through the clouddrive:// URL scheme.

  • File Provider
  • clouddrive:// external player

Media

Cinema-grade playback, straight from the cloud

A 60 GB disc image never has to come down to your phone. The player reads from the cloud, and decoding, menus, audio tracks and subtitles all come with it.

Emby & Jellyfin client with Direct Stream

Connect your own Emby or Jellyfin servers and browse libraries with posters, cast and Continue Watching. When your CloudDrive can reach the same file, Direct Stream plays it straight from the cloud — bypassing the server transcoder for full-quality playback of formats servers can't transcode, while watch history stays in sync.

  • Direct Stream
  • Watch history in sync

A serious media player

Hardware-accelerated decoding of H.264, H.265/HEVC and AV1, with HDR10, HDR10+ and Dolby Vision — including Dolby Vision playback of Profile 7 Blu-ray content. Picture-in-Picture, per-track audio and subtitle delay, subtitle styling and HDR subtitle brightness are all built in.

A Profile 7 disc carries two picture layers and CloudDrive2 reads both, but dual-layer output needs a device that supports it. On most devices the picture arrives as single-layer Dolby Vision — still Dolby Vision, not an HDR10 fallback.

  • Dolby Vision P5 / 7 / 8
  • HDR10+ · HLG
  • Picture-in-Picture

Blu-ray ISOs with real disc menus

Play a Blu-ray ISO directly from cloud storage and navigate the native disc menu: scene selection, coloured shortcut keys, in-menu audio and subtitle setup, even 2D+3D combo discs.

Disc menus cover HDMV discs. On iPhone and iPad a BD-J (Java) disc plays the main feature directly; for BD-J menus, use Windows, the macOS direct download, Android or Meta Quest.

  • HDMV menus
  • 2D + 3D combo discs
  • Scene selection

AI subtitle translation

Generate translated subtitles for any video. CloudDrive2 translates the existing subtitle track first — including PGS bitmap subtitles read by on-device OCR — and falls back to speech recognition when there is none. Use Apple's on-device translation models, which are free and keep the audio on your device, or bring your own API. Generated subtitles are saved next to the video, ready to reuse on your other devices.

  • Apple on-device models
  • PGS OCR
  • Saved for reuse

Multichannel, Dolby Atmos and audiophile DSD

Dolby Atmos (Dolby Digital Plus) bitstreams to your AVR over HDMI. TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio and DTS decode in full and reach multichannel USB DACs, HDMI adapters and AirPlay 2 as 5.1 or 7.1 — never folded down to stereo. AirPods Pro and Max get head-tracked spatial audio instead.

DSD files (.dsf, .dff) and SACD ISOs stream straight from the cloud, including true 5.1 multichannel DSD, and DoP output feeds compatible DACs bit-perfectly. Lock the screen and the music keeps playing.

DSD64 through DSD1024 are all supported. How far DoP and Native DSD actually go is set by the DAC you connect — the app outputs to what the DAC itself reports, and anything beyond that is converted to PCM.

  • DSD64 → DSD1024
  • SACD ISO
  • TrueHD · DTS-HD MA
  • Background playback

A multi-gigabyte PDF, open in seconds

The streaming PDF reader never pulls the whole book down. It fetches only the byte range the pages on screen actually need, so a multi-gigabyte art book typically goes from tap to first page in seconds — no waiting for a download, and no complete copy left on the device.

Turning pages, zooming and jumping through the outline all fetch on demand. Page thumbnails, bookmarks and reading-position memory are all there.

  • Fetches pages on demand
  • No full download
  • Page thumbnails
  • Bookmarks and position

More

The rest of what you'll use daily

  • Movie library metadata

    Reads your local .nfo files and poster, fanart and logo artwork in Plex, Jellyfin and Emby naming. No online scraping — your library stays private.

  • Cross-cloud transfers

    Copy or move files between providers. The transfer manager handles pause, resume, retry, throttling and concurrency, and uploads whole folders recursively.

  • Backup and sync

    Scheduled, automated backup tasks, plus whole photo-library backup to the cloud.

  • Security

    Two-factor authentication, encrypted folders and session monitoring, with API tokens that grant scoped, expiring access.

  • A WebDAV server of its own

    Your iPhone or iPad is itself a WebDAV server: every cloud you have connected is served over a standard protocol, so third-party apps on the same network — players, file managers, backup tools — read your cloud content directly without signing in to each provider again.

  • And these

    Disk cache with configurable eviction for offline access, four view modes, opening files in Infuse, VLC or nPlayer, background audio, dark and light themes, English and Chinese interfaces.
