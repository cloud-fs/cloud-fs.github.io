How it’s wired in

No sign-up: the device registers itself

A certified device leaves the factory carrying a unique device ID, and CloudDrive2 builds the account straight from it — the sign-up step disappears. The resulting account is missing nothing: it is exactly the same as one created with an email address.

  1. The device ships with an ID

    Every certified device carries a unique device ID, registered with us before it leaves the factory.

  2. First launch creates the account

    Open CloudDrive2 and it creates the account from that ID — no email address, no verification code. We record the device as a Trusted Device.

  3. Claim it with your own email whenever you like

    On the Profile page, Change Email and Password swaps in your own address and password in one step. After that the account signs in on your phone, computer and TV too.

A device-ID account needs a real username and email before it can be deleted

Deleting an account requires an address we can verify it against, so a device-ID account has to fill in a username and email on the Profile page first. That same step is what makes the account properly yours.

Three hooks into Xunlei

  • Sign in with a Xunlei account

    Xunlei users don’t register separately. The CloudDrive2 sign-in page has a Xunlei button under Or sign in with; authorise once and you’re in. The resulting account behaves like any other — same membership, same cloud configuration, same backup tasks.

  • Built into the Xunlei app

    Xunlei has integrated CloudDrive2 into its own client, so the interface opens inside Xunlei with nothing separate to install. In that mode CloudDrive2 recognises where it is running and hides the entry points you no longer need.

  • Downloads accelerated by Xunlei

    Select files in My Files and click Download with Xunlei: the job is handed to Xunlei’s download engine and runs at whatever speed its acceleration gets you. Pick several files at once and they arrive as one task group named after the current folder.

Work with us

We’re open to every manufacturer

NAS, TV box, media player, headset — if you want CloudDrive2 in your product, come talk to us. Here’s what a formal partnership gets you.

  • Device-ID registration

    Your devices create accounts from their own IDs, so your users are up and running on first boot with no sign-up and no email verification.

  • Certification and a place on this page

    Your users can confirm that the CloudDrive2 on their device is the officially integrated build, not something bundled in by hand.

  • Integration support

    Preinstallation, launch arguments, Docker and Linux deployment, and the gRPC API if you want to drive CloudDrive2 from your own interface.

  • Hardware bring-up

    Remote-control UI, decoder capabilities, mount permissions — take the hardware-specific problems straight to us instead of through the community.
