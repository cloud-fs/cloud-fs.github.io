中文版

如何请求删除账户

你可以随时请求删除 CloudDrive2 账户。请从你的注册邮箱发送邮件至 waytechcloudfs@gmail.com， 主题写「删除账户 / Delete my account」，正文注明你的账户邮箱。

我们会先向该邮箱发送确认邮件核验身份，确认后处理删除请求。请求通常在 7 个工作日内处理完成。

你也可以在应用内操作：设置 → 账户 → 删除账户，该入口会打开本页面。

删除后会移除的数据

  • 你的账户记录：邮箱地址、加密后的密码、两步验证设置
  • 会员 / 订阅状态与权益记录
  • 与账户关联的 API 令牌与登录会话

删除后会保留的数据

  • 交易记录：为满足税务与财务法规要求，购买凭证会以最小化形式保留（法律要求的期限内，通常为 5 年），并与你的身份信息解除关联。
  • App Store / Google Play / Meta Horizon Store 侧的购买记录由相应平台保存，我们无法删除；请向对应平台申请。

不受影响的数据

你各家云盘中的文件不会因为删除 CloudDrive2 账户而被删除——我们从不存储你的文件。云盘凭证保存在你的设备本地，卸载应用即被清除。删除账户前请自行确认云盘数据已妥善保管。

注意

删除账户不可撤销。已购买的会员 / 订阅权益会一并失效且不予退款；如你有正在生效的订阅，请先在 App Store / Google Play / Meta Horizon Store 中取消订阅，避免继续扣费。

English

How to request deletion

You can request deletion of your CloudDrive2 account at any time. Email waytechcloudfs@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my account", stating your account email in the message.

We will send a confirmation email to that address to verify your identity, then process the deletion. Requests are normally completed within 7 business days.

You can also start from inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete account, which opens this page.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account record: email address, encrypted password, two-factor settings
  • Membership / subscription status and entitlement records
  • API tokens and login sessions tied to the account

Data that is retained

  • Transaction records. Purchase receipts are retained in minimised form for as long as tax and financial regulations require (typically 5 years), disassociated from your identity.
  • Purchase records held by the App Store, Google Play or the Meta Horizon Store belong to those platforms and cannot be deleted by us — please contact them directly.

Data that is unaffected

Deleting your CloudDrive2 account does not delete any files in your cloud services — we never store your files. Cloud credentials live locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. Please make sure your cloud data is safe before requesting deletion.

Please note

Account deletion cannot be undone. Any purchased membership or subscription entitlement is forfeited and is not refundable. If you have an active subscription, cancel it in the App Store, Google Play or the Meta Horizon Store first to avoid further charges.
