Delete your CloudDrive2 account

Last updated: 29 July 2026

中文版

如何删除账户

在应用内删除（推荐，立即生效）

打开 CloudDrive2，进入设置 → 账户 → 注销我的账户。iPhone、iPad、Mac、 Apple TV、Vision Pro、Android 手机 / 平板 / 电视、Meta Quest 以及网页版均已支持，全程在应用内完成。

  1. 应用会先说明哪些数据将被删除，然后向你的注册邮箱发送一次性确认码（30 分钟内有效）。
  2. 输入该确认码与你的账户密码；如果你开启了两步验证，还需输入两步验证码。
  3. 输入 DELETE 确认。账户立即删除且无法撤销，没有缓冲期。

请先取消正在生效的订阅。只要还有自动续期的订阅在生效，账户就无法删除。订阅只能由你本人 在购买它的商店（App Store / Google Play / Meta Horizon Store）中取消，我们无法代为取消。

如果你的账户还有余额，删除时需要你明确确认放弃该余额，余额不予退款也无法转移。

无法登录时

如果你无法登录应用，请从你的注册邮箱发送邮件至 waytechcloudfs@gmail.com， 主题写「删除账户 / Delete my account」，正文注明你的账户邮箱。我们会先向该邮箱发送确认邮件核验身份， 确认后处理删除请求，通常在 7 个工作日内完成。

删除后会移除的数据

  • 你的账户记录：用户名、邮箱地址、加密后的密码及个人资料
  • 两步验证密钥与恢复码
  • 会员 / 订阅状态、App Pro 权益与推荐码
  • 与账户关联的 API 令牌、全部登录会话，以及保存在账户中的云盘授权令牌与绑定关系

已绑定的设备会被释放（解除绑定，而非删除），之后可重新初始化设置。你的邮箱地址也会被释放， 日后可用它重新注册新账户。

删除后会保留的数据

  • 交易记录：为满足税务与财务法规要求，购买凭证会以最小化形式保留（法律要求的期限内，通常为 5 年），并与你的身份信息解除关联。
  • App Store / Google Play / Meta Horizon Store 侧的购买记录由相应平台保存，我们无法删除；请向对应平台申请。

不受影响的数据

你各家云盘中的文件不会因为删除 CloudDrive2 账户而被删除——我们从不存储你的文件。云盘凭证保存在你的设备本地，卸载应用即被清除。删除账户前请自行确认云盘数据已妥善保管。

注意

删除账户不可撤销，没有缓冲期。已购买的会员 / 订阅权益会一并失效且不予退款。

通过 App Store / Google Play / Meta Horizon Store 购买的终身版（一次性买断）可以在日后 注册的新账户上通过「恢复购买」重新绑定；自动续期订阅则必须在删除账户前于对应商店中取消。

English

How to delete your account

In the app (recommended — takes effect immediately)

Open CloudDrive2 and go to Settings → Account → Delete my account. This is available on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Vision Pro, Android phone / tablet / TV, Meta Quest and the web app, and the whole process happens inside the app.

  1. The app first explains exactly what will be deleted, then emails a one-time confirmation code to the address registered on your account (valid for 30 minutes).
  2. Enter that code and your account password. If you have two-factor authentication enabled, enter your two-factor code as well.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account is then deleted immediately and cannot be recovered — there is no grace period.

Cancel any active subscription first. Your account cannot be deleted while an auto-renewing subscription is still running. A subscription can only be cancelled by you, in the store where you bought it (App Store, Google Play or the Meta Horizon Store) — we cannot cancel it for you.

If your account still holds a balance, you will be asked to explicitly confirm that you are giving it up. Forfeited balance cannot be refunded or transferred.

If you cannot sign in

If you are unable to sign in to the app, email waytechcloudfs@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my account", stating your account email in the message. We will send a confirmation email to that address to verify your identity, then process the deletion — normally within 7 business days.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account record: username, email address, encrypted password and profile details
  • Your two-factor secret and recovery codes
  • Membership / subscription status, App Pro entitlement and referral code
  • API tokens, every sign-in session, and any cloud-service tokens and bindings stored with your account

Bound devices are released (detached, not deleted) and can be set up again from scratch. Your email address also becomes available again, so you can register a new account with it later.

Data that is retained

  • Transaction records. Purchase receipts are retained in minimised form for as long as tax and financial regulations require (typically 5 years), disassociated from your identity.
  • Purchase records held by the App Store, Google Play or the Meta Horizon Store belong to those platforms and cannot be deleted by us — please contact them directly.

Data that is unaffected

Deleting your CloudDrive2 account does not delete any files in your cloud services — we never store your files. Cloud credentials live locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. Please make sure your cloud data is safe before requesting deletion.

Please note

Account deletion cannot be undone and there is no grace period. Any purchased membership or subscription entitlement is forfeited and is not refundable.

A lifetime (one-off) purchase made through the App Store, Google Play or the Meta Horizon Store can be re-bound to a new account later using "Restore Purchases". An auto-renewing subscription, by contrast, must be cancelled in that store before the account can be deleted.
